Common Questions

What's the difference between Quick Reset and Comprehensive?
Quick Reset generates ~50 core belief pairs covering your open centers at a high level. Comprehensive goes gate-by-gate, generating ~200 deeply-specific pairs tailored to every activated gate in your chart. Most people start with Comprehensive because the specificity is where the transformation lives.
Do I need to know Human Design to use this?
No. The generator uses your birth data to compute your BodyGraph internally. The workbook explains each section in plain language — you don't need any prior HD knowledge. If you're already a student of HD, the gate-level specificity will go deeper than most chart-reading apps.
How is this different from a generic affirmation app?
Generic affirmations aren't rooted in your specific conditioning. Belief Deprogrammer maps each open center in your chart to the specific limiting belief that open center amplifies, then pairs it with the embodied truth for your activated gates. The specificity is what makes the work land.
Is my birth data private?
Yes. Birth data is used only to compute your BodyGraph in real time and is never stored, logged, or shared. The generator is stateless — your data lives only in your browser session.
What if I'm not satisfied?
The Comprehensive workbook comes with a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If the workbook doesn't resonate with your design, email us within 30 days for a full refund. The 1:1 coaching tier includes a complementary discovery session first so you can decide if the fit is right before committing.
Can I re-generate after a major life event?
Yes. Comprehensive tier includes lifetime re-generations. Your birth data doesn't change, but your embodied awareness does — running a fresh workbook after a Saturn return, a relationship shift, or a spiritual opening often reveals new layers.
