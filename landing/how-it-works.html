From Birth Data to Personalized Workbook

Four steps. Two minutes. A workbook written specifically for the design you came in with.

01

Enter Your Birth Data

Date, time, and location of birth. These three pieces of information are enough to compute your full Human Design BodyGraph — your Type, Strategy, Authority, Profile, all 9 Centers, all 36 Channels, all activated Gates, and your Incarnation Cross.

  • Most accurate with birth time (from birth certificate or family memory)
  • Birth location determines your exact planetary positions
  • Privacy-first: data is never stored, only used to compute in real-time
What you don't need: any prior Human Design knowledge. The workbook explains everything in plain language as you read.
02

The Engine Computes Your BodyGraph

Your birth data is sent to the Belief Deprogrammer API, which calls the Human Design Engine to compute your complete chart in real time. The result is a structured graph of your design — which centers are defined, which are open, which gates are activated, and which channels are complete.

  • Birth data → planetary positions (via standard ephemeris)
  • Positions → gate activations (Human Design's I-Ching wheel)
  • Gate activations → center definitions & channel completions
  • Full chart → passed to the gate-aware belief generator
Stateless by design: the API receives your birth data, computes the workbook, returns it. Nothing is logged, stored, or shared. Your chart lives only in your browser session.
03

The Generator Maps Beliefs to Your Specific Design

For every open center in your chart, the generator surfaces the conditioning pattern that open center amplifies. For every activated gate, it surfaces the specific belief and the embodied truth. The result is a workbook that couldn't be written for anyone else — because no one else has your combination.

  • Open centers → the wisdom-portal and the conditioning it amplifies
  • Activated gates → the specific limiting belief + the embodied truth
  • Complete channels → the theme and a weekly practice sequence
  • Type, Authority, Profile → contextual framing throughout
Two depth tiers: Quick Reset (~50 belief pairs) for a high-level overview. Comprehensive (~200 belief pairs) for the full gate-by-gate deconditioning path.
04

Read, Practice, Integrate

Your workbook is ready in seconds. Download it as Markdown for editing, plain text for journaling, or paste into Notion. Read one belief pair a day. Practice the embodiment prompt. Notice the patterns. Return to it after major life shifts for a fresh layer.

  • Read one belief + truth pair per day
  • Sit with the embodiment prompt for a week
  • Journal what arises — body reactions, dream images, recurring thoughts
  • After 90 days, regenerate and notice what's shifted
Embodiment, not just insight: insight alone doesn't rewire anything. The workbook pairs every belief with a body-based practice so the new pattern can land in your nervous system, not just your mind.

What A Typical Journey Looks Like

📅

Day 1: Generate & Read

You generate your workbook, read through the first 5-10 belief pairs, and notice which ones land hardest. Those are your entry points.

📅

Week 1-2: Identify the Active Pattern

One belief pair per day, journaled. Notice what triggers the old pattern in real-time — situations, people, body sensations.

📅

Week 3-6: Practice the New Pattern

Use the embodiment prompts. Catch the old pattern. Practice the new response. Small, daily, embodied repetitions.

📅

Week 7-12: Regenerate & Go Deeper

After 90 days, regenerate your workbook. The conditioning patterns that were loud before are often softer. New layers surface. Repeat the cycle.

The Science Underneath

Belief Deprogrammer isn't a replacement for therapy — it's a complement. The methodology sits at the intersection of three research areas, each grounded in peer-reviewed work.

Memory Reconsolidation

Bruce Ecker, Robin Ticic, Laurel Hulley — when an emotional memory is reactivated in a context that disconfirms it, the memory becomes temporarily plastic. New information can be encoded in the same window.

Somatic Intelligence

Bessel van der Kolk, Peter Levine, Resmaa Menakem — trauma and limiting beliefs are stored in the body, not just the mind. Body-based cues are how we catch patterns in real-time.

Epigenetics

Bruce Lipton, Joe Dispenza — gene expression responds to belief and perception. The body differentiates between a real threat and a remembered one only when the nervous system updates.

ACT & Defusion

Steven Hayes, Russ Harris — Acceptance and Commitment Therapy's defusion techniques (observing a thought rather than being inside it) are core to the embodiment protocols.

Parts Work

Richard Schwartz (IFS), Bradley Nelson (EFT/tapping) — the workbook treats each limiting belief as a protective part with a positive intention. Working with the part, not against it.

Cognitive Flexibility

Diana Deutsch, George Lakoff — reframing a belief requires experiencing an alternative, not just being told. Each workbook pair gives the alternative a body-based practice.
