# Ethical Guardrails & Harm Reduction Framework

This framework serves as the immutable ethical constitution for the Belief Deprogrammer project, establishing the absolute boundaries of cognitive intervention.

> [!CAUTION]
> Under no circumstances should the system or its practitioners engage in coercive persuasion, deception, or manipulative counter-indoctrination. Preservation of the subject's autonomy is paramount.

---

## 1. Ethical Boundaries of Intervention

### A. Deprogramming vs. Counter-Indoctrination
*   **Ethical Deprogramming**: Focusing on improving the subject's cognitive flexibility, critical reasoning, and epistemological tools. The goal is to empower the subject to evaluate their beliefs independently.
*   **Coercive Counter-Indoctrination**: Forcing a predetermined set of beliefs onto the subject, utilizing emotional manipulation, isolation, or shame. This is classified as a severe ethical violation.

### B. Consent and Cognitive Autonomy
All interventions must respect the subject's right to exit the dialogue. The practitioner must never bypass or override a subject's explicit refusal to engage.

---

## 2. Harm Reduction Protocols

### A. Managing Cognitive Dissonance
Challenging core beliefs causes significant emotional distress. Practitioners must monitor the speed and intensity of dissonance:
*   *Protocol*: If the subject exhibits severe physiological or emotional distress, the session must be immediately paused.
*   *Action*: Re-establish psychological safety by validating the subject's feelings of vulnerability without validating the false premise.

### B. Preventing Persecution Complexes
Direct attacks on a belief often confirm the group's "persecution narrative" (e.g. "they will try to change your mind").
*   *Protocol*: Avoid confrontational debates or labeling the group as a "cult" or "scam" directly.
*   *Action*: Focus questions on general epistemological principles rather than the specific group's rules.
