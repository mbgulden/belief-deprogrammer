# Cognitive Bias and Fallacy Index

This index aggregates, categorizes, and deconstructs the psychological mechanisms that sustain entrenched, maladaptive belief systems.

> [!NOTE]
> Understanding the cognitive barriers to change is the first step in designing non-coercive intervention vectors.

---

## 1. Deconstructed Cognitive Biases

### A. Confirmation Bias
*   **Operational Definition**: The tendency to search for, interpret, favor, and recall information in a way that confirms one's prior beliefs or values.
*   **Psychological Origins**: Evolutionary pressure to minimize cognitive effort and preserve psychological safety within tribal/social structures.
*   **Mechanism in Belief Formation**: Acts as an information filter, systematically discarding contradictory data while amplifying supporting anecdotes.
*   **Real-World Example**: A subject in an online conspiracy echo chamber who registers only the failed predictions of mainstream media while ignoring similar or worse failures in alternative media.
*   **Methodological Countermeasure**: Socratic questioning (see [Methodologies Playbook](./deprogramming_methodologies_playbook.md)), specifically highlighting information processing inconsistencies.
*   **Detailed Node**: A dedicated conceptual node is available at [Confirmation Bias](./cognitive_biases/confirmation_bias.md) (exploring its core operational definitions).

### B. The Backfire Effect
*   **Operational Definition**: A cognitive phenomenon where presenting corrective facts actually increases the subject's belief in the original misconception.
*   **Psychological Origins**: Identity protection. When a core belief is challenged, the brain perceives it as a threat to personal survival.
*   **Mechanism in Belief Formation**: Challenging data triggers flight-or-fight mechanisms, leading to defensive rationalization and doubling down on the belief.
*   **Real-World Example**: Showing scientific consensus reports to a vaccine skeptic, who then concludes the scientific community is part of a coordinated cover-up.
*   **Methodological Countermeasure**: Street Epistemology (see [Methodologies Playbook](./deprogramming_methodologies_playbook.md)), prioritizing epistemological foundation analysis over factual debate.
