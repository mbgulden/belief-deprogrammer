The muscle testing foundation of Nelson's work is classified as **pseudoscience** by major medical organizations:
- American Cancer Society: No evidence for diagnosis/treatment
- Australian Govt Review (2015): "No clear evidence of effectiveness"
- Multiple double-blind studies: AK "no more useful than random guessing"
